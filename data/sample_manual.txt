X200 INDUSTRIAL PUMP MAINTENANCE MANUAL

1. GENERAL INFORMATION

The X200 industrial pump is designed for continuous fluid-transfer operations.
Only trained technicians should perform installation, inspection, or maintenance.

Disconnect electrical power and release system pressure before beginning any
maintenance procedure.

2. PUMP HOUSING

The X200 pump housing contains the impeller, shaft assembly, and mechanical seal.

During reassembly, inspect the housing for cracks, corrosion, and deformation.
The housing mating surfaces must be clean and free from old gasket material.

The X200 pump housing bolts must be tightened to 45 newton-metres.

Tighten the housing bolts gradually using a diagonal sequence. Do not tighten
one bolt completely before tightening the remaining bolts.

3. LUBRICATION

Inspect the bearing lubrication every 500 operating hours.

Use ISO VG 68 industrial lubricant under normal operating conditions.

Do not mix different lubricant types because incompatible lubricants may reduce
bearing life.

4. TROUBLESHOOTING

If the pump produces excessive vibration, inspect the shaft alignment, mounting
bolts, bearings, and impeller.

Low flow may be caused by an obstructed inlet, an incorrectly rotating motor,
a worn impeller, or air entering the suction line.

If leakage appears around the shaft, inspect the mechanical seal for damage or
incorrect installation.

5. SAFETY

Wear eye protection and suitable gloves while servicing the pump.

Never operate the pump without the protective coupling guard installed.

Do not perform maintenance while the pump is connected to electrical power.
